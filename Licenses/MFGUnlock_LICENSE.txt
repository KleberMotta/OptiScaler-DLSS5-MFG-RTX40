DLSS Multi Frame Generation unlock for RTX 40 (OptiScaler/mfgunlock)
=====================================================================

The in-memory patching code under OptiScaler/mfgunlock/ is a port of the
"MFG Unlock" ReShade addon:

  https://github.com/mavismmg/MFGAdaUnlock-RenoDx   (fork maintained by mavismmg)
  https://github.com/ImDreamt/MFGAdaUnlock-RenoDx   (original addon by Dreamt)

The underlying technique -- the Ada arch-gate rewrite, the temporal-midpoint
correction of the DLSS-G interpolation kernel and the Streamline wrapper
verification -- originates from dashdogy's RTX40MFG-Unlock:

  https://github.com/dashdogy/RTX40MFG-Unlock

Both projects are published under the MIT License, reproduced below.
